Motivoa — Privacy Policy

Effective Date: September 18, 2026 · Last updated: September 18, 2026

1. Who we are

Motivoa (“the App”) is published by TubeAI Labs (“we”, “us”), an independent app developer based in the United Kingdom. For the purposes of the UK GDPR and the EU GDPR, we are the data controller for the limited data described below.

Contact: motivoa.app@gmail.com

2. The short version

Motivoa is local-first. There is no account, no login, and no server of ours holding your writing. Your affirmations, journal entries, mood check-ins, voice recordings and conversations with the companion are stored only on your device.

Three things do leave your device, and only in the circumstances described below: anonymous analytics and crash reports (if you consent), an advertising request (free version only), and a report you explicitly choose to send us about something the AI companion said.

3. What we collect, and what we don't

Stays on your device — never sent to us

  • Your name, if you choose to enter one
  • Custom affirmations, journal entries, evidence notes and reflections
  • Mood check-ins and emotion words
  • Voice recordings you make in the app
  • Your conversations with the AI companion
  • Search queries, streaks, journeys and preferences (categories, reminder times, theme)
  • Any therapy summary or export you generate

These are held in the App's private storage and are not transmitted to us for any purpose.

Collected only with your consent

DataPurposeWho receives it
Anonymous usage events (which features are opened) Understanding which features are used Google Firebase Analytics
Crash logs and performance diagnostics Finding and fixing crashes Google Firebase Crashlytics
App-instance identifier Grouping the above without identifying you Google Firebase

You are asked on first launch and can change your answer at any time in Settings. If you decline, analytics and crash reporting are off. No usage event ever carries anything you wrote — not a mood value, not a journal entry, not a search query.

Collected in the free version, for advertising

See section 5. In summary: your device's advertising ID and general technical information are sent to Google to request an ad, and this is shared with Google for advertising purposes. Premium removes every ad and this data flow with it.

Sent only when you explicitly ask us to

If the AI companion says something you want to report, the App offers a “report this response” control. Tapping it shows you exactly what will be sent and asks you to confirm. Only then do we receive:

  • The companion's reply you are reporting
  • A short note you may optionally type
  • The report category you picked, the time, the App version and your language

Nothing else is attached — no name, no device identifier, no other part of your conversation, and nothing that links the report back to you. We use these reports solely to investigate unsafe or inappropriate AI output and to improve the App's safety filters. Reports are retained for up to 24 months and then deleted.

4. The AI companion

Motivoa includes an AI companion. How it works matters for your privacy, so here it is plainly:

  • It runs on your device. Generated replies come from an on-device model (Google's ML Kit GenAI / Gemini Nano). What you type to the companion is processed on your phone and is not sent to us or to any model server.
  • On devices without on-device AI support, the companion answers from a fixed set of written responses instead. Either way, nothing you write leaves the device.
  • Generated text is always labelled in the App as AI-generated. The companion is not a human, and it is not a therapist.
  • Access to the generative features is restricted to adults (18+) and requires your consent.
  • The only way anything from a companion conversation reaches us is the explicit report described above.

5. Advertising

The free version shows ads supplied by Google AdMob. Premium removes them everywhere.

Where ads appear: at the foot of the Today view and the Categories list, and as an occasional full-screen card between affirmations in the scrolling feeds — never more often than one card for every three affirmations. They never appear in your journal, your mood check-in, your evening letter, your companion's chat, or any support or crisis screen, and nothing takes over the screen and waits to be dismissed.

What is shared to show them: to request an ad, the Google Mobile Ads SDK sends your device's advertising ID and general technical information (device type, operating system, and coarse location derived from your IP address) to Google. Nothing you write is ever sent — your affirmations, journal entries, moods, voice recordings and companion conversations are never used to target advertising.

Your choices:

  • Where consent is required (the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), you are asked before any ad is requested, through Google's User Messaging Platform. Nothing is requested from AdMob until you have answered, and you can change your answer at any time from the App's privacy settings.
  • If you are in a US state with an applicable privacy law, the App offers you an opt-out from the sale or sharing of your data for personalised advertising, reachable in the same place.
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in Android's Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Buying Premium removes every ad from the App.

Google's handling of this data is described in Google's Privacy & Terms.

7. Third parties

We do not sell your personal information for money, and we never share the content you write. Some US state privacy laws define “sharing” broadly enough to include personalised advertising; where that applies to you, use the opt-out described in section 5.

The App uses these Google services:

  • Firebase Analytics & Crashlytics — anonymous usage and crash data, only with your consent
  • Firebase Remote Config — fetches configuration to your device; nothing you write is sent with the request
  • Google Play Billing — processes Premium purchases. We never see your payment details
  • Google AdMob — ads in the free version (section 5)
  • Google User Messaging Platform — asks for and records your ad consent choice
  • ML Kit GenAI (on-device) — runs the companion's model locally; sends nothing

8. International transfers

The Google services above are operated by Google and may process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Google provides safeguards for these transfers, including the UK and EU Standard Contractual Clauses and the EU–US Data Privacy Framework.

9. Retention

  • On-device content — kept until you delete it or uninstall the App. We hold no copy.
  • Analytics and crash data — retained by Google under Firebase's default retention (up to 14 months for analytics; 90 days for crash reports).
  • Reports you send us — up to 24 months, then deleted.

10. Security

Your content stays in the App's private storage, which Android isolates from other apps. Any data that does leave the device is sent over encrypted HTTPS connections; the report path refuses to send to anything that is not a secure connection. No system is perfectly secure, and because your data lives on your device, keeping your device locked and up to date is an important part of protecting it.

11. Your rights

In the App, at any time:

  • Edit or delete any entry
  • Turn analytics on or off in Settings
  • Review or change your ad consent choice, where one applies
  • Export or back up your data
  • Erase everything (see section 12)

If you are in the UK, the EEA or Switzerland, you also have the right to access, correct, erase, restrict or object to processing, to data portability, and to withdraw consent at any time. Because we hold almost nothing that identifies you, most of these are exercised directly in the App. For anything else, write to motivoa.app@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days. You also have the right to complain to your data protection authority — in the UK, the Information Commissioner's Office.

If you are in California or another US state with an applicable privacy law, you have rights to know, delete, correct, and to opt out of the sale or sharing of personal information, and we will not discriminate against you for exercising them.

12. Deleting your data

Motivoa stores your data on your device only — there is no account and no server-side copy.

To erase everything: open Motivoa → Profile → App Settings → Danger Zone → “Delete all my data.” This permanently removes affirmations, journal entries, moods, streaks, voice recordings, journeys, companion history and settings from the device. Uninstalling the App also removes its data.

To request deletion of a report you sent us, or of analytics data associated with your app instance, email motivoa.app@gmail.com.

13. Children's privacy

Motivoa is not directed to children. It is intended for users aged 13 and above, and the AI companion's generative features are restricted to users aged 18 and above. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child under 13 has used the App, contact us and we will act on it.

14. Not medical advice

Motivoa supports wellbeing; it does not provide medical or mental-health treatment, diagnosis or advice, and it is not a crisis service. If you are in crisis or in danger, contact your local emergency services.

15. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top, and — for any change that materially affects your privacy — tell you in the App before it takes effect.
